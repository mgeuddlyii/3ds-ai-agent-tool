FAQ (1)

Nothing recognised?

Retry slower and make sure no other program holds the mic. Too short a recording shows the hold-ZL hint.

Key rejected (401)?

The key is invalid, expired, or out of quota. Try another, or switch in the key manager.

Connection failed?

Read the reason on the top screen first. The 3DS TLS stack is old; some modern providers may fail the handshake - switch to a tested provider.

Zero hits in guide search?

Use shorter keywords and check the limited game range. If the vocabulary load failed message shows, restart and retry.

CIA will not install, error 0xD8E08027?

The title version is older than the installed one. Uninstall the old build or overwrite it with FBI; versions only go up.

Mic unusable?

The screen points to status.txt diagnostics; follow the lines there.
