Buttons

  • D-pad / C-Stick: move focus; Home is a grid.
  • A: confirm / send.
  • B: back to the previous screen.
  • X: jump to Settings from Home.
  • Y: open the system keyboard, common to most screens.
  • SELECT: fill a preset phrase / cycle an option.
  • Hold ZL: voice input.
  • START: easter egg key, no function promised.
  • Touch screen: every button can be tapped.
