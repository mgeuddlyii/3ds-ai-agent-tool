Home

Home holds the core entries only, and shows config status: credential, this session's safety level, theme.

Buttons

  • Chat: the chat screen, first row, big tile.
  • Guides: search the built-in game FAQ, second row.
  • More: a submenu of 6 items, second row.
  • Settings: top right, press X or touch to enter.

Controls

  • Move in the grid with the D-pad or C-Stick, A confirms.
  • Every button can be tapped on the touch screen.
  • Settings is outside the grid: press X or tap it.
