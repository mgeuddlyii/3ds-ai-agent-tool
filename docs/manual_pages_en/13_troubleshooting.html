Troubleshooting

Symptom table

  • Home shows no credential: check key.txt sits under SD:/3ds/aiconsole/, then the credential status in Providers.
  • Spinning forever after send: wait for the provider; on timeout it returns to chat - see connection diagnostics in status.txt.
  • Voice does nothing: hold ZL, speak for a full 0.5 s, check mic diagnostics in status.txt.
  • Guide answers look mixed up: keywords too broad, narrow the game range.
  • No icon after install: .cia needs a tap in FBI; look in the cias/ folder.
  • Settings change has no effect: theme applies at once; safety level is per session - restart the app.
