Offline Guides

Built-in guide search: no network, no key.

Coverage

  • FAQ and walkthrough passages for 211 games.
  • Hits are printed verbatim - no made-up answers.
  • A built-in micro model (1.18M params) plus BM25, all on the card.

How to use

  1. Open Offline Guides from Home.
  2. Press Y to open the keyboard and type a question.
  3. Results show at once; you can limit the game range.

Status notes

If it shows "vocabulary load failed - search unavailable", restart and retry; if it persists, check the diagnostic lines in SD:/3ds/aiconsole/status.txt.
