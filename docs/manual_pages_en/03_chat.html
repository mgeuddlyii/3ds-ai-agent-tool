Chat

Basics

  • Top screen is the conversation; bottom screen is input and send.
  • Press Y to open the system keyboard.
  • Press SELECT to fill a preset phrase.
  • Hold ZL to speak; release to end capture.
  • Under 0.5 s shows: Recording under 0.5 s - hold ZL, then speak.
  • B mid-way returns to Home; history is kept.

Action confirm

AI replies may carry action markers. A confirm screen pops up: full facts on top, only allow / deny below. Nothing runs unless you confirm.

Translate mode

Turn it on from the first More item before chatting; replies become Chinese. It is a chat mode, not a new screen.

Common states

  • Nothing recognised - try again, slower.
  • No audio captured - check the mic is not in use.
  • Connection failed - reason on the top screen.
  • Key rejected (401) - invalid or expired; try another one.
