FAQ (2)

Why no stereoscopic 3D?

Old 3DS does not support it. On New 3DS the toggle is in Settings; stereo rendering is not enabled in this build, the switch is reserved.

How do I move games from my PC to the card?

More - PC files. That is the only entrance.

What if the model asks me to do something dangerous?

The action layer does not accept anything outside the allowlist. The confirm screen states the full facts; when unsure, deny.

Update froze halfway?

The update screen has staged progress; an interrupt cannot corrupt the original - verify first, install after.

Why are there no pictures in this manual?

The built-in viewer is an HTML subset with no image support. Artwork lives in the repo docs; in-app display needs a separate texture mechanism.
