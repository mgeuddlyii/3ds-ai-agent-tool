About AIConsole

AIConsole is an AI console that runs on the 3DS, and an AI game assistant.

It does three things

  • Chat: sends what you say or type to a cloud LLM; replies show on the top screen.
  • Act: performs 4 fixed action types inside the safety gates; every step needs your confirmation.
  • Offline guides: searches the built-in FAQ of 211 games, no network, no key.

It is not a chat toy - the point is the action layer. The model cannot see your files; every path is checked locally.

Forms

  • .3dsx: launch from the Homebrew Launcher.
  • .cia: install with FBI, launch from the HOME icon.

Models

New 3DS / New 3DS LL: full features. Old 3DS: runs, but stereo 3D and eye tracking are unavailable.

First launch

4 unskippable pages: language, capability limits, NAND backup reminder, AI service notes.
